Kiswahili

KukuPaja ("sisi", "yetu") ni programu ya simu inayounganisha wateja na wafugaji/mabroker wa kuku Tanzania. Sera hii inaeleza taarifa tunazokusanya na jinsi tunavyozitumia.

Taarifa Tunazokusanya

  • Jina kamili, namba ya simu, na eneo unapoishi (wakati wa kujisajili)
  • Taarifa za oda: bidhaa ulizonunua, anwani ya kupelekewa, njia ya malipo, na jina la mlipaji
  • Ujumbe kati yako na broker/admin ndani ya programu
  • Token ya kifaa chako kwa ajili ya kutuma arifa (notifications)

Jinsi Tunavyotumia Taarifa

  • Kukamilisha na kufuatilia oda zako
  • Kuwasiliana nawe kuhusu akaunti au oda yako
  • Kuboresha huduma za KukuPaja
  • Kutuma arifa muhimu (mfano: hali ya oda)

Kushiriki Taarifa

Hatutauza taarifa zako kwa mtu au kampuni yoyote. Taarifa za oda yako zinashirikiwa tu na broker/mfugaji aliyepangiwa kukamilisha oda hiyo, na admin wa KukuPaja kwa ajili ya uthibitisho.

Usalama wa Taarifa

Nywila zako zinahifadhiwa kwa njia ya usimbaji (encrypted). Mawasiliano kati ya programu na server yanapitia HTTPS.

Haki Zako

Unaweza kuomba kuona, kubadilisha, au kufuta taarifa zako wakati wowote kwa kuwasiliana nasi kupitia barua pepe hapa chini.

Watoto

KukuPaja haikusudiwi kutumiwa na watoto walio chini ya miaka 18 bila ridhaa ya mzazi/mlezi.

English

KukuPaja ("we", "our") is a mobile marketplace app connecting customers with poultry brokers/farmers in Tanzania. This policy explains what information we collect and how we use it.

Information We Collect

  • Full name, phone number, and location (at sign-up)
  • Order details: items purchased, delivery address, payment method, and payer name
  • Messages exchanged with brokers/admin within the app
  • Device token used to send push notifications

How We Use Information

  • To fulfill and track your orders
  • To communicate with you about your account or orders
  • To improve KukuPaja's services
  • To send you relevant notifications (e.g. order status updates)

Sharing of Information

We do not sell your information to any third party. Order information is shared only with the broker/farmer assigned to fulfill that order, and with KukuPaja admins for verification purposes.

Data Security

Passwords are stored encrypted. Communication between the app and our servers is secured with HTTPS.

Your Rights

You may request to view, correct, or delete your data at any time by contacting us at the email below.

Children

KukuPaja is not intended for use by children under 18 without parental/guardian consent.
